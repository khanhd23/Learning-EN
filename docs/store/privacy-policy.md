# EngPet Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

EngPet ("the app") is an English-learning app with a virtual pet, published on Google Play by
Đặng Kim Khánh ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. If you have
a question, write to khanhsphg2003@gmail.com.

## The short version

- You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
- Your learning progress stays on your phone. We do not have a server and we never receive it.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure
  ads, as described below.
- The microphone is used only for speaking practice, only when you tap the microphone button, and
  the app does not record or keep your voice.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The app stores your lessons, answers, review schedule, test history, pet, coins, quests and
settings in its private storage on your phone. This information is not sent to us or to anyone
else by the app.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app's data in
your device backup, and may copy it to a new phone when you set one up. Google handles that
backup under your Google account; we cannot see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's
settings.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads, and the Google User Messaging Platform to ask
for your consent where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United
Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states).

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and send to Google:

- your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
- the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
- interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
- diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance.

This information is sent over an encrypted connection. Where consent is required, ads are
personalised only if you agree; otherwise Google shows non-personalised or limited ads. You can
change your choice at any time from the app's settings (privacy options), and you can reset or
delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

We ask Google to show only ads suitable for a general audience (maximum ad content rating "G").
Rewarded ads are always optional.

Learn how Google uses this information:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 3. Microphone (speaking practice)

When you tap the microphone in a speaking exercise, the app asks for microphone permission (once)
and passes your speech to your phone's speech recognition service to turn it into text. On many
phones this service is provided by Google and may process the audio on Google's servers under
Google's own privacy policy. The app compares the text with the sentence you practised and then
discards it. The app does not record, save or upload audio. You can refuse or remove the
permission at any time; every other part of the app still works.

## 4. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone. Reminders are
created on the device and no information is sent anywhere.

## 5. Internet use

Lessons, dictionary, stories and tests work offline. The app uses the internet only to load ads
and the consent form.

## 6. Children

EngPet is made for learners aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

## 7. Your choices and deleting data

- Learning data: use "Clear storage" for the app in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app.
  This deletes everything the app stored on your device.
- Advertising: change your consent in the app's privacy options, and reset or delete your
  advertising ID in your phone's settings.
- Microphone and notifications: turn the permissions off in your phone's settings.

Because we do not collect personal information on our own servers, there is nothing for us to
delete on our side. For data collected by Google for ads, see Google's privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 8. Security

The app's data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Ad data is
sent to Google over encrypted connections.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes,
tell you in the app.
